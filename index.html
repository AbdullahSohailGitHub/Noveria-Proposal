“Recruit reps, win B2B partners, and generate qualified leads — three audiences, one site.”

The build

Three distinct paths built into the navigation and forms from day one, each routed to its own place in your CRM, not bolted on after launch.

Your brief

“An easy-to-manage blog — publishing articles without friction.”

The build

A headless CMS your team opens like a doc editor — the same pattern already shipped on Memento Payments, detailed below.

Your brief

“Entirely in Spanish, performing well on desktop, tablet and mobile.”

The build

Spanish-first copy structure baked into the CMS, plus a responsive pass tested down to a 320px screen — not just a breakpoint check.

Stack specification

The categories Stripe and Linear are built on, sized for Noveria.

RENDERING

Next.js 14, App Router — server-rendered for the fast first paint and strong Google.es ranking a new brand needs.

CONTENT

Headless CMS (Contentful) — your team publishes the blog and edits copy without ever opening a code editor.

STYLING & MOTION

Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion — the scroll-reveal and micro-interaction feel from your reference sites, tuned to run smoothly.

HOSTING

Vercel Edge Network — consistent load times whether a visitor opens the site in Madrid, Barcelona, or Valencia.

LEAD ROUTING

Native forms, wired to a webhook — rep applications and partner inquiries land wherever your team already works, no extra inbox to check.

Why this doesn't stop at launch

“If this collaboration is successful, we expect to work together again on future digital projects.”

That's the collaboration on offer here, not a one-off build. Daily updates, overlap with your working hours, and a single developer who already knows the codebase when the next project — a partner portal, an internal dashboard, a second market — comes up.

No handoff to a new contractor. No re-explaining the brief from scratch.
